# 8.1 Overview

Purchase Documents is where every invoice you receive lives - commercial invoices, credit notes, corrective invoices and the rest.

Your role here is the reverse of the sales side. You did not create these documents and you do not send them. What you do is **review and decide**: acknowledge, approve, dispute, suspend or refuse. Your decision is exchanged back with your supplier, and where the mandate requires it, reported to the PPF.

Three things happen here:

- **You receive.** Invoices arrive over the Peppol network from your supplier's PA. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is submitted.

- **You track.** Each document carries two independent statuses, as on the sales side.

- **You decide.** Every action available to you is a response to a document someone else issued.

Purchase Documents is split into three tabs - **e-Invoicing**, **e-Reporting** and **Self Billed**. This chapter covers the **e-Invoicing** tab and how to read it. Self-billed invoices reverse the flow, so they are covered on their own at the end.

Reviewing and responding to an invoice has its own chapter after this one.

# 8.2 Getting there

From the e-Invoicing dashboard, click the **Purchase Documents** icon in the left sidebar.

# 8.3 The three tabs

| **Tab** | **What it holds** |
|----|----|
| **e-Invoicing** | Domestic B2B invoices received from your suppliers through the Peppol network |
| **e-Reporting** | International B2B purchases, reported to the PPF rather than exchanged with a supplier's platform |
| **Self Billed** | Self-billed invoices you have raised on behalf of your suppliers |

| **WARNING Filters, summary cards and actions apply only to the tab you are on.** If a document seems to be missing, check that you are on the right tab as well as the right entity. |
|----|

Click **Learn more** above the table to open the status info panel, which lists every status along with its definition.

*[F51] Status info panel, buyer view*

Everything the panel contains is reproduced below.

## 8.4.1 Invoice Status in full

**Arriving**

| **Status** | **What it means** |
|----|----|
| **Received** | Your PA received the invoice from your supplier's platform and is processing it for availability. This happens at platform level - you will not normally see it. |
| **Made Available** | A new invoice has arrived from your supplier and is ready for you. This is the first status you see. Review the invoice and take action - approve, dispute, suspend or refuse. |

**Your decisions**

| **Status** | **What it means** |
|----|----|
| **Acknowledged** | You are reviewing this invoice |
| **Approved** | You approved this invoice |
| **Disputed** | You flagged a problem with this invoice. Waiting for your supplier to resolve it. |
| **Suspended** | You requested additional documents from your supplier. Waiting for them to provide them. |
| **Completed** | Your supplier provided the additional information you asked for after your suspension. Review and decide - approve or dispute. |
| **Cancelled** | Your supplier cancelled this invoice after you disputed it. A corrective invoice will arrive separately. |
| **Refused** | You permanently refused this invoice. Reported to the PPF. Terminal - no further action. |
| **Rejected** | Your platform automatically rejected this invoice on arrival because of validation errors. Reported to the PPF. |

**Payment**

Payment statuses are set by your supplier when they confirm collection, and appear here so you can see where payment stands. **There is no payment action on your side.**

| **Status** | **What it means** |
|----|----|
| **Partial Payment Collected** | Your supplier confirmed collection for part of the invoice. The remaining amount will be reported as it is collected. |
| **Partial Payment Collected with Failures** | Your supplier collected part of the invoice, but some collections failed to report to the PPF. Waiting for them to report the failed amounts again. |
| **Payment Collected** | Your supplier confirmed payment received. Lifecycle complete. |
| **Payment Failed** | Reporting the collected payment to the PPF failed. Waiting for your supplier to report the payment again. |

## 8.4.2 Government Reporting in full

Government Reporting shows a single header-level status summarising the filings required for that document. Hover over it to expand the individual flows.

| **Status** | **What it means** |
|----|----|
| **Pending** | No filing to the PPF has been attempted yet |
| **In Progress** | Reports have been transmitted to the PPF and are awaiting confirmation. Some may already be accepted while others are still in flight. |
| **Accepted** | Every required report has been accepted by the PPF. Nothing further is needed. |
| **Failed** | At least one required filing was rejected by the PPF and could not be resolved automatically |

| **NOTE You report less than your supplier does.** Your supplier files the invoice metadata and the submitted lifecycle status. The only lifecycle events reported from your side are **Rejected** and **Refused**. Everything else you do - acknowledging, approving, disputing, suspending - is exchanged with your supplier but not filed with the PPF. |
|----|

# 8.5 Summary cards

Four cards sit above the table. Each shows a live count, and clicking one filters the table to that status.

| **Card** | **What it counts** |
|----|----|
| **Total Received** | Every invoice that has arrived from your suppliers |
| **Rejected** | Turned away automatically on arrival because of validation errors |
| **Refused** | Invoices you permanently refused |
| **Payment Collected** | Invoices your supplier has confirmed as paid in full |

| **TIP There is no card for what is waiting on you.** Filter Invoice Status to **Made Available** to see the invoices that have arrived and not yet been acted on. |
|----|

# 8.6 Summary view

The summary view is how the list presents your documents by default: one row per invoice, carrying a condensed set of fields drawn from the document itself.

| **Column** | **What it shows** |
|----|----|
| **Invoice Number** | Your supplier's document number |
| **Invoice Status** | Current lifecycle status |
| **Government Reporting** | Header-level reporting status - hover to expand individual flows |
| **Invoice Date** | The date your supplier issued the invoice |
| **Invoice Type** | Commercial invoice, credit note, corrective invoice and so on |
| **Invoice Received Date** | The date the invoice arrived with you |
| **Seller Name** | Your supplier |
| **Seller SIREN** | Your supplier's registration number |
| **Invoice Currency Code** | The currency the invoice was issued in |
| **Total Amount (incl. VAT)** | Gross value |
| **Total VAT Amount** | VAT component |

## 8.6.1 The two status columns

**Invoice Status** and **Government Reporting** track two separate journeys. Every document carries both at once, and they move independently:

| **Column** | **What it tracks** | **Who drives it** |
|----|----|----|
| **Invoice Status** | Where the document is in its lifecycle - arrived, reviewed, decided, paid | You and your supplier |
| **Government Reporting** | Whether the filings required for that document have been accepted by the PPF | ClearTax and the PPF |

![[F52] Two Status Columns - Invoice Status & Govt. Reporting](/images/image51.png)

*[F52] Two Status Columns - Invoice Status & Govt. Reporting*

| **WARNING Two status names appear in both columns and mean different things: Pending and Failed.** Under Government Reporting they refer to the state of a filing, not the state of the invoice. Always check which column you are reading. |

# 8.7 Filtering the list

Click **Filter** to open the filter panel.

![[F53] Filter panel, Purchase Documents](/images/image50.png)

*[F53] Filter panel, Purchase Documents*

Filters are grouped into sections. Use the **Search Filters** box at the top to jump straight to a filter, or scroll through the panel.

## 8.7.1 Filter groups

| **Filter** | **What it does** |
|----|----|
| **Invoice Date** | Filter by the date your supplier issued the invoice |
| **Invoice Received Date** | Filter by the date the invoice arrived with you |
| **Invoice Number** | Free-text search on the invoice number |
| **Seller SIREN** | Free-text search on your supplier's SIREN |
| **Invoice Status** | Filter by any of the statuses listed in the status info panel above |
| **Invoice Type** | Filter by document type - commercial invoices, credit notes, corrective, prepayment, factored and self-billed variants |

## 8.7.2 Applying and clearing filters

At the bottom of the panel:

- **Apply** - applies your selections. The table refreshes, and each active filter appears as a chip above the table.

- **Clear All** - resets every filter in the panel back to unselected.

- **×** (top-right of the panel) - closes the panel without applying your changes.

Individual filter groups have their own **Clear** link, which resets only that group.

# 8.8 Acting on documents

One control above the table lets you act on the documents in the list: the **Actions** dropdown.

| **WARNING There is no Submit button here.** You did not issue these documents, so there is nothing to send. Everything you do is a response. |
|----|

## 8.8.1 The Actions dropdown

Select one or more invoices in the list, open **Actions**, and choose what you want to do.

![[F54] Actions dropdown, open](/images/image25.png)

*[F54] Actions dropdown, open*

Actions fall into two groups.

**Invoice actions** record your decision on the document and exchange it with your supplier.

| **Action** | **What it does** | **Input needed** |
|----|----|----|
| **Acknowledge** | Marks the invoice as under review | None - single click |
| **Approve** | Confirms the invoice is correct | None - single click |
| **Dispute** | Flags a problem for your supplier to resolve | Reason required |
| **Suspend** | Asks your supplier for additional documents | Reason and comment required |
| **Refuse** | Permanently rejects the invoice | Reason and comment required |

Each of these is covered step by step in the chapters that follow.

## 8.8.2 What you can do, and when

| **WARNING Not every action is available on every document.** What you can do depends on the document's current status. |
|----|

Which actions are available to you at each status.

| **Status**            | **What you can do**                            |
|-----------------------|------------------------------------------------|
| **Made Available**    | Acknowledge, Approve, Dispute, Suspend, Refuse |
| **Acknowledged**      | Approve, Dispute, Suspend, Refuse              |
| **Approved**          | Nothing - your decision is recorded            |
| **Disputed**          | Approve, Refuse                                |
| **Suspended**         | Approve, Refuse                                |
| **Completed**         | Approve, Dispute                               |
| **Refused**           | Nothing - terminal                             |
| **Rejected**          | Nothing - terminal                             |
| **Payment Collected** | Nothing - terminal                             |

| **TIP A dispute or suspension does not lock you in.** From either status you can still approve or refuse the invoice. |
|----|

| **NOTE Refused, Rejected and Payment Collected are terminal.** The document is closed and no further action is possible. |
|----|

## 8.8.3 Export actions

Export actions produce a copy of the document for use outside ClearTax.

| **Action** | **What it does** |
|----|----|
| **Email** | Sends a PDF copy of the invoice to a recipient you choose |
| **Print** | Generates a PDF for download |
| **Download** | Exports the document data - XML Report, Lite Report or Detailed Report |

## 8.8.4 Adjusting the table

Two further controls sit alongside these and change how the list is displayed rather than the documents themselves:

- **Show / hide and reorder columns** - choose which columns appear in the summary view and in what order

- **Sort** - order the table by any sortable column

## 8.9.2 Finding out what went wrong

The header of the detailed view carries the invoice number and the current document status.

Where a document has issues, a banner sits directly above the document body, with a count of how many were found. **Click View error details to drill into the full detail behind it.**

Issues come in two kinds on this side.

| **Issue type** | **What it means** | **Where it came from** |
|----|----|----|
| **Rejection details** | Your platform turned the invoice away on arrival because of validation errors | Your platform |
| **Government reporting issues** | A filing to the PPF failed, with the reason given | The PPF |

Neither can be fixed by editing, because you did not issue the document. Where the invoice itself is wrong, the remedy is to dispute or refuse it and let your supplier correct it.
