# 1.1 Overview

ClearTax France gives you two home screens, and it's worth knowing which is which before you start.

The **Clear Finance Cloud landing page** is the outer shell. You land here when you log in, and it's the launcher for every ClearTax product your organisation uses - you'll pass through it on your way into e-Invoicing.

The **e-Invoicing dashboard** is the working home page inside the app itself. This is where you'll spend almost all of your time.

This section walks through both surfaces so you know what every control does before you send your first invoice.

# 1.2 The Clear Finance Cloud landing page

![[F01] Clear Finance Cloud landing page](/images/image45.png)

*[F01] Clear Finance Cloud landing page*

## 1.2.1 The header bar

The header runs across the top of the page and stays with you as you move around.

| **Element** | **What it does** |
|----|----|
| **App switcher** (⋮⋮⋮, top-left) | Returns you to this landing page from anywhere in the product |
| **Demo workspace banner** (yellow strip) | Tells you that you're working in a demo workspace. Live workspaces don't show this banner - if you can see it, nothing you do here is real. |
| **EN language toggle** | Switches the interface between English and French |
| **Bell icon** | System notifications |
| **Workspace pill** (top-right, e.g. "ClearTax") | Opens your account and workspace menu |

## 1.2.2 Switching language

Click **EN** in the header to switch the interface between English and French. The change applies immediately and follows you into e-Invoicing.

![[F02] English / French toggle, open](/images/image58.png)

*[F02] English / French toggle, open*

## 1.2.3 Your account and workspace menu

Click the workspace pill in the top-right corner to open your account panel.

![[F03] Workspace and account menu, open](/images/image52.png)

*[F03] Workspace and account menu, open*

From here you can see and do the following:

- **Your avatar and workspace name**, confirming who you're signed in as

- **Your account email** - the address you signed up with

- **Account & Security settings** - password, multi-factor authentication and active sessions

- **Your workspaces** - every workspace you belong to. Your current workspace is marked accordingly; click **Manage** on any workspace to open its settings.

- **Log out of all workspaces**

If you work across more than one legal entity or environment, this menu is how you move between them.

## 1.2.4 Product cards

The main area of the page shows a tile for each ClearTax product you're licensed for.

- **e-Invoicing** - *"Generate E-Invoices within minutes."* Click this tile to enter the e-Invoicing dashboard.

As you add products to your workspace, they appear here alongside it.

# 1.3 The e-Invoicing dashboard

Click the e-Invoicing tile and you arrive at your working dashboard.

![[F04] e-Invoicing dashboard](/images/image40.png)

*[F04] e-Invoicing dashboard*

## 1.3.1 The top bar

Most of the top bar behaves exactly as it does on the landing page, with two additions specific to e-Invoicing.

| **Element** | **What it does** |
|----|----|
| **App switcher** (⋮⋮⋮) | Returns you to the Clear Finance Cloud landing page |
| **SIREN / SIRET selector** | Shows the entity you're currently working in. Click to switch entity or branch. |
| **EN language toggle** | Switches between English and French |
| **Support Desk** | Raises a support ticket with ClearTax without leaving the product |
| **Bell icon** | Notifications specific to e-Invoicing |
| **Workspace pill** | The same account menu as the landing page |

## 1.3.2 Choosing the entity you're working in

Everything on the dashboard is scoped to a single entity or branch, so this selector controls what you see everywhere else.

Click the SIREN / SIRET pill to open the entity switcher. Use the search box to filter by SIREN, SIRET or branch name.

![[F05] Entity and branch switcher, open](/images/image14.png)

*[F05] Entity and branch switcher, open*

The dropdown lists:

- **Parent entities**, identified by SIREN. Click the arrow beside an entity to expand its branches.

- **Branches**, identified by SIRET. Select a branch to narrow the dashboard to that branch's documents.

Every list, count and summary card on the dashboard rescopes to whatever you select here.

| **WARNING If a document you expect to see is missing, check this selector first.** You may be looking at a different entity or branch. |
|----|

## 1.3.3 The left sidebar

The sidebar is collapsed to icons by default. Hover over it, or click the expand icon, to reveal the labels.

![[F06] Left sidebar, expanded](/images/image11.png)

*[F06] Left sidebar, expanded*

| **Label** | **Where it takes you** |
|----|----|
| **Home** | Back to this dashboard |
| **Sales Documents** | Everything you issue to your buyers - B2B sales, international sales e-reports, and self-billed invoices you've received |
| **Purchase Documents** | Everything you receive from your suppliers - B2B purchases, international purchase e-reports, and self-billed invoices you've issued |
| **Import** | Bulk upload of documents from a file |
| **Import History** | A record of every past upload and how it went |
| **Reports** | Business insights and analytics |
| **Settings** (bottom) | Workspace, users, integrations and notification preferences |

## 1.3.4 Announcements

The right-hand rail carries platform-wide announcements from the ClearTax team: product releases, release notes, and notices about incidents or planned downtime. When there's nothing to report, you'll see a sleeping "Z" icon and the message *"No announcements yet. New announcements will appear here."*

Worth glancing at when something behaves unexpectedly - a known incident will usually be posted here first.

## 1.3.5 Help and support

Two tiles sit at the bottom of the dashboard:

| **Tile** | **What it does** |
|----|----|
| **Product guides and videos** | Opens the guides library - including this document |
| **Product release notes** | The latest features, fixes and improvements |
