# 6.1 Overview

When a payment lands in your bank, ClearTax does not know about it. There is no bank connection - you tell ClearTax, using the **Confirm Payment** action.

Confirming a payment does two things at once: it reports the collection to the government, and it shares the updated status with your buyer's PA.

| **WARNING Payment reporting applies to services invoices only.** Where VAT is due on collection rather than on issue, the payment must be reported. For other invoices there is nothing to report. |
|----|

# 6.2 Before you start

Three things are worth knowing before you open the modal, because each one catches people out.

| **Rule** | **Why it matters** |
|----|----|
| **Report in EUR** | Payments must be reported in euros, whatever currency the invoice was issued in. This is mandated by the PPF, not a ClearTax choice. |
| **Split by VAT rate** | You report the amount collected broken down by VAT rate, not as a single figure. |
| **Payment Collected is terminal** | Once the full amount is confirmed, the lifecycle is complete and no further collections can be recorded against that invoice. |

# 6.3 When you can confirm a payment

The action becomes available as soon as the invoice has been delivered successfully to your buyer's PA. It can be taken from any of these statuses:

- **Received**

- **Made Available**

- **Acknowledged**

- **Approved**

You do not need to wait for your buyer to approve the invoice before recording a payment against it.

# 6.4 How to confirm a payment

**1. Open the Actions dropdown and choose Confirm Payment.**

![[F39] Confirm Payment in the Actions dropdown](/images/image65.png)

*[F39] Confirm Payment in the Actions dropdown*

**2. Check the figures ClearTax has filled in.** The modal shows the total amount for collection, the amount still due, and how much has been collected so far, along with the VAT rates taken from the invoice.

![[F40] Confirm Payment modal, showing details from the invoice](/images/image27.png)

*[F40] Confirm Payment modal, showing details from the invoice*

**3. Enter what you collected, split by VAT rate.** Put the amount received against each rate. Remember this must be in EUR.

![image42](/images/image42.png)

***\[F41** Entering the amount against each VAT rate*

**4. Confirm.** ClearTax reports the collection to the government and exchanges the updated status with your buyer's PA. The invoice status updates to reflect what you recorded.

# 6.5 Collecting in more than one payment

Payments often arrive in installments, and ClearTax handles that. Record each one as it arrives.

After a partial collection, the invoice status becomes **Partial Payment Collected**.

![image55](/images/image55.png)

***\[F42** Invoice showing Partial Payment Collected*

When you come back to record the next instalment, the modal already knows what you collected last time and shows the remaining balance due.

![[F43] Second collection, showing the balance due from the previous payment](/images/image13.png)

*[F43] Second collection, showing the balance due from the previous payment*

Enter the new amount against each VAT rate as before. In this example the remaining balance is collected in full.

![[F44] Entering the second payment, clearing the balance](/images/image66.png)

*[F44] Entering the second payment, clearing the balance*

Once the whole amount has been collected, the status becomes **Payment Collected**.

![[F45] Full payment collected](/images/image43.png)

*[F45] Full payment collected*

| **WARNING Payment Collected is terminal.** No further collections can be recorded against that invoice. |

# 6.6 When ClearTax cannot fill in the balance

For most invoices, ClearTax works out the balance due for you. Two cases prevent that, and both need you to supply the figures yourself.

| **Case** | **Why ClearTax cannot calculate it** | **What you do** |
|----|----|----|
| **Prepayment invoices** | ClearTax does not know the VAT-rate split of the amount already prepaid | Enter the split yourself, from your ERP or business system |
| **Invoices issued in a currency other than EUR** | ClearTax does not know the exchange rate you applied when the payment was received | Convert to EUR yourself and enter the split |

| **WARNING In these two cases the figures are yours to get right.** ClearTax cannot check them against the invoice, so take the split from your ERP or business records rather than estimating. |
|----|

# 6.7 Payment history

Because payments can arrive in instalments, ClearTax keeps a record of every collection you report against an invoice.

Click **Payment History** in the summary view to open the payment history drawer on the right of the screen.

![[F46] Payment history view](/images/image33.png)

*[F46] Payment history view*

The drawer lists every payment made and reported for that invoice. For each one it shows the details of the collection and its current state on both fronts: **delivery of the update to your buyer's PA**, and the **outcome of reporting it to the PPF**. The two are tracked separately, exactly as they are for the invoice itself.

![[F47] Payment history showing the VAT-rate split](/images/image46.png)

*[F47] Payment history showing the VAT-rate split*

| **NOTE The statuses used here are the payment statuses from the status info panel** - Partial Payment Collected, Payment Collected, Payment Failed and the rest. See the status info panel in the Sales Documents chapter for the full definitions. |
